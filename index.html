Our story

Neighbourhood coffee, made slowly

We started with one secondhand roaster and a stubborn belief that a coffee shop should feel like a living room. Ten years later we still roast in small batches, still know most names at the counter, and still believe the best cup is the one you have time to finish.

Whether you're meeting friends, getting some work done, or simply enjoying a quiet break, there's a seat with your name on it.

"Stop by, grab your favourite drink, and make yourself at home."
— The Ember & Oak crew
